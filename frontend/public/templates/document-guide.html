24Rx KYC Document Format Guide

Complete Guide for Vendor/Trader Registration

📋 Document Requirements

All documents must be uploaded in PDF, JPG, or PNG format with a maximum file size of 10MB.

1. Basic Business Documents

* GST Registration Certificate

  • Must be a valid GST registration document
  • Should clearly show company name and GSTIN
  • Document should not be expired

* PAN Card

  • Company or Individual PAN card
  • Clear, legible scan or photo
  • All corners visible

* Factory License / Panchayath / Corporation Certificate

  • Valid factory operating license
  • OR Panchayat/Municipal Corporation trade license
  • Must show business address

Optional FSSAI Certificate

  • Required if dealing with food/nutraceutical products
  • Must be valid and not expired

* Cancelled Cheque

  • Cheque with company name
  • Clearly showing bank name, account number, IFSC code
  • Must have "CANCELLED" written across it

* Indemnity Certificate

  • On company letterhead
  • Signed and stamped by authorized signatory
  • Indemnifying 24Rx against risks

* Drug License 1 (Mandatory)

  • Valid drug license for wholesale/retail
  • Must show license number and validity
  • Should cover pharmaceutical trading

Optional Drug Licenses 2, 3, 4

  • Additional drug licenses if applicable
  • For different categories or locations

2. Authorization & Quality Documents

* Manufacturer Authorization Letter

  • Letter from manufacturer authorizing you to sell their products
  • On manufacturer's letterhead
  • With seal and signature

Optional Agreement with Manufacturer

  • Formal agreement document
  • Showing business relationship terms

Optional Quality Certifications (FDA, CE, etc.)

  • Any quality certifications you hold
  • FDA approval certificates
  • CE marking certificates
  • ISO certifications

* Certificate of Incorporation

  • Company registration certificate
  • From ROC (Registrar of Companies)
  • Showing company details and registration number

Optional MSE Certificate

  • Micro and Small Enterprise certificate
  • If applicable to your business

Optional Udyog Aadhar

  • Udyog Aadhar registration
  • For MSME businesses

Optional NSIC/KVIC/UAM Certificate

  • National Small Industries Corporation registration
  • Khadi and Village Industries Commission certificate
  • Udyam Registration (formerly UAM)

3. Legal & Compliance Documents

* Non-Conviction Certificate

  • Certificate from local police
  • Stating no criminal convictions
  • For all directors/partners

Optional Supply Order

  • Recent purchase orders from clients
  • Proof of business activity

Optional Sample Declaration Form

  • If providing product samples
  • Declaration of sample authenticity

* Declaration Form

  • General declaration of compliance
  • On company letterhead
  • Signed by authorized signatory
  • Declaring adherence to 24Rx policies

📌 Important Notes

  • * indicates mandatory documents
  • All documents must be clear and legible
  • Expired documents will be rejected
  • Documents must match the registered business name
  • Scanned copies or clear photos are acceptable
  • File size must not exceed 10MB per document

🔄 Review Process

After submission:

  1. Your documents will be reviewed by our admin team
  2. Review typically takes 24-48 hours
  3. You'll receive an email notification once approved or if any corrections are needed
  4. Login credentials will be sent to your registered email
